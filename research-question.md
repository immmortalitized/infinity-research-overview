# Research question

**Can persistent structured epistemic state improve long-horizon evidence tracking and belief revision relative to strong alternative memory representations under controlled conditions?**

The question concerns how a system retains evidence and revises beliefs over time. The proposed comparison asks whether an explicit representation of epistemic state offers an advantage over strong alternatives, rather than assuming that persistence or structure alone is sufficient.

## Hypothesis under investigation

The hypothesis motivating INF-EXP-0001 is that a bundled structured-state treatment may improve evidence tracking and belief revision under the tested conditions. This is a hypothesis for evaluation, not an established finding. No empirical H2 claim has been made.

The intended comparison must account for strong alternative memory representations and fair comparator treatment. Apparent improvements could otherwise reflect differences in reasoning support or access to information rather than the intended treatment.

## Evidence boundaries

Any future positive result would support only the tested bundled structured-state treatment in the tested setting. It would not isolate every component's contribution or establish adaptive orchestration, substrate independence, self-improvement, or the broader Infinity thesis.

A negative or inconclusive result would remain valid research evidence. The planned work includes deliberate attempts to falsify the hypothesis and identify alternative explanations.
