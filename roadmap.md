# Planned roadmap

The following steps are plans, not completed milestones. Infinity remains in engineering and methodological validation, and no empirical H2 claim has been made.

1. Clarify the narrow research question and the limits of conclusions that the proposed comparison could support.
2. Establish evaluator isolation, review comparator fairness, and satisfy methodological gates. Real-model testing remains intentionally blocked until these requirements are met.
3. Version the methodology and deliberately attempt to falsify the hypothesis, including consideration of strong controls and alternative explanations.
4. After the gates are satisfied, conduct the planned controlled comparison and report outcomes with their limitations. Negative and inconclusive results remain valid.
5. Before any applications or outreach, complete a crash course and oral-defense preparation phase covering the question, comparator rationale, methodology, evidence limits, and potential failure modes.

Applications and outreach are contingent on that preparation phase. This roadmap makes no claim that preparation, validation, testing, applications, or outreach have occurred.

Any future positive result would apply only to the tested bundled structured-state treatment. It would not establish adaptive orchestration, substrate independence, self-improvement, or the broader Infinity thesis.
