# Research status

Status as of October 5, 2026: **engineering and methodological validation**.

No empirical H2 claim has been made. This repository reports no completed real-model evaluation, performance metrics, or empirical finding.

## Real-model testing gate

**Real-model testing is intentionally blocked until evaluator isolation, comparator fairness, and methodological gates are satisfied.**

This status does not imply that those gates have passed. Work toward establishing and reviewing them precedes real-model testing and empirical interpretation.

## Plans and evidence

INF-EXP-0001 remains a planned controlled comparison at the conceptual level described in the [experimental design](experimental-design.md). Strong controls, fair comparators, versioned methodology, and deliberate attempts to falsify the hypothesis are requirements for the planned research, not completed validation claims.

Negative results are valid. Inconclusive outcomes must also be represented as inconclusive.

## Limits of future conclusions

A future positive result would apply only to the tested bundled structured-state treatment and tested setting. It would not establish adaptive orchestration, substrate independence, self-improvement, or the broader Infinity thesis.

The [roadmap](roadmap.md) describes planned next steps. This public overview contains no private implementation, experiment assets, or unpublished data.
